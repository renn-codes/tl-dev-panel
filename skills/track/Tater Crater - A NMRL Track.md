# Tater Crater - A NMRL Traack
Using any available assets among all of the MPQs recursively in the provided WoW folder, design me over an appropriate amount of ADTs, a racetrack for the NO MERCY Racing League module. It is named "`Tater Crater`". It should include a full set of heightmaps along with matching texture alphamaps to import into Noggit Red (and the appropriated textures to assign), and using whatever gameobjects neccessary to flesh out the track path and decorating the landscape as appropriate. Ensure that all gameobjects used have the correct rotation and height where they are being implemented. The track should have 4 laps, and a average lap traversal length of 1.15 minutes.

## Track Environemnt and Layout
The track runs counter-clockwise along the inside edge of the a live volcano's caldera, about halfway from the lava's surface and the top of the crater's opening. The track varies in height as it travels along in an twisting, overall oblong shape. There shoould be a few steep gaps from lava-rivers and lava-falls, spanned with rail-less wodden bridges and/or platforms for some challenge. There should be minimal guardrails on the inner edge of the track bordering the lava crater, ocurring sporadically on the straighter sections of track. The cliff bordering the lava crater is very steep and runs directly to the lava's surface. The outer edge of the track should run along a steep-walled rise to the top of the crater, far enough up to block any view of outside the volcano. The entirety of the track, except a portion around the starting gate, is subject to ocurrences of the `BOOM! Barrel` hazard. 

It will be on it's own map, with the top-left-most tile starting at `1,1`. You may add decorative, theme-appropriate gameobjects and some flying, environment-appropriate creatures to the map for flavor. There should also be a few horde blimps that slowly move high above the crater in the sky, in continuous patterns.

## Track-Specific Mechanics
Use the following for track hazards:
- **Lava:** If a player falls into lava, after 1 second, they should be moved to the checkpoint they passed through, and have to reaccelerate rather than keep their prior speed. The players should not take damage from lava while they are racing on this track, and lava damage should be restored as normal once the race completes and they are teleported from the track to their prior locations.
- **BOOM! Barrels:** At random intervals, and in matches of scattered, random locations long the entirety of the track (except an excluded area around the starting gate), goblin-themed explosive barrels fall from the sky ontop the track and explode on hitting the ground. If they hit a player, the explosion tosses them a random, but fair, distance in a random direction from their current position. After being tossed, if the player does not land in lava (use that appropriate effect on the player if they land there), the player's speed is reset and they must start accelerating again. If able, add AOE target indicators to where the barrels are to land once they spawn and start falling to the track.
