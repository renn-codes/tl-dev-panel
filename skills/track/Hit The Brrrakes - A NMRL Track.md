# Hit The Brrrakes - A NMRL Traack
Using any available assets among all of the MPQs recursively in the provided WoW folder, design me over an appropriate amount of ADTs, a racetrack for the NO MERCY Racing League module. It is named "`Hit The Brrrakes`". It should include a full set of heightmaps along with matching texture alphamaps to import into Noggit Red (and the appropriated textures to assign), and using whatever gameobjects neccessary to flesh out the track path and decorating the landscape as appropriate. Ensure that all gameobjects used have the correct rotation and height where they are being implemented. The track should have 3 laps, and a average lap traversal length of 2.0 minutes.

## Track Environemnt and Layout
The track is a Northrend-themed, many island traversal track themed with snow and ice. The starting island should be somewhat larger than the rest of the many islands, and have very steep edge to near vertical transistions into the water, used appropriately per island composition.  Wooden bridges and/or platforms, and broken ice platforms/pieces should join the islands along the track. There should be 2 sections of the track that consist of the "`Ice Breakers` hazard, described in detail in the next section. Use a snowing weather effect for this track's environment, and lock the time to later-daytime, as it will be in it's own separate map. It's top-left-most tile should start at `1,1`. You may add decorative islands, landscape features, and gameobjects as appropriate. Include 3 horde blimps that fly above the track at an appropriate height, moving along a somewhat circular set route.

## Track-Specific Mechanics
Use the following for track hazards:
- **Water:** If a player falls into water, after 1 second, they should be moved to the last checkpoint they passed through, and have to reaccelerate rather than keep their prior speed.
- **Ice Breakers:** These sections of track should be a somewhat wider expanse of track connecting two islands, and appropriate length each of scattered ice platforms, large ice pieces, and Icecrown-themed plaforms. These objects should be able to be jumped between, but still cause the player to fall in the water if the jump is missed. You may put powerups as normally places along the track in `Ice Breakers` sections.
  - For one of the `Ice Breakers` sections, it should be a bit larger then the other two, with the objects making up this section of track being spread in a way that the players can have a few different paths that they can take to the next island, with varying efficiency each.
  - For the other of the `Ice Breakers` sections, the track made of objects should curve a moderate distance over 65-degrees as it connects two islands.
