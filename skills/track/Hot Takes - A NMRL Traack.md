# Hot Takes - A NMRL Traack
Using any available assets among all of the MPQs recursively in the provided WoW folder, design me over an appropriate amount of ADTs, a racetrack for the NO MERCY Racing League module. It is named "`Hot Takes`". It should include a full set of heightmaps along with matching texture alphamaps  after to import into Noggit Red (and the ppropriated textures to assign), and using whatever gameobjects neccessary to flesh out the track path and decorating the landscape as appropriate.

## Track Environemnt and Layout
The track is a volcanic, lava floes and rivers canyons themed track. The track should be traveling in the steep walled, branching canyon, and wind through it. It should run mostly next to somwehat deeper than track level lava rivers, and at times crossing them over wooden bridges and platforms. There should be two lightly hairpinned turns, and sections of the track that have no guard-rails, to add extra challenge to the players.

## Track-Specific Mechanics
If a player falls into lava, after 1 second, they should be moved to the checkpoint they passed through, and have to reaccelerate rather than keep their prior speed. The players should not take damage from lava while they are racing on this track, and lava damage should be restored as normal once the race completes and they are teleported from the track to their prior locations.
