# Prompt
How do I let the password limit be 8 characters rather than 10?

# Reponse
Lowered the minimum password length for panel accounts from 10 to 8 characters (v1.8.1).

- The limit is now one constant, `Passwords.MinLength = 8`, in `src/TeamLigma.Panel.Server/Auth/Crypto.cs`. It applies everywhere passwords are set: first-run setup, My Account, the Users page and the `user-reset` command.
- The "10+ characters" hints on the sign-in, My Account and Users pages now say 8, and so does the README.
- Existing passwords keep working; the limit only applies when a password is set.

**Tested:** `user-reset` refused a 7-character password ("Passwords must be at least 8 characters.") and accepted an 8-character one. Both parts compile.
